Why Monad

A trust check has to be cheap and fast
enough to run before every job.

~1 second finality

In the demo, approve and open each confirmed within about a second; release likewise.

ERC-8004 already deployed

Canonical Identity + Reputation registries on Monad testnet: the passport mirrors every settlement into them.

USDC + x402 native

Circle USDC with EIP-3009 (gasless hires) and an x402 facilitator for pay-per-call verification.

P256 precompile

A hirer can release payment with a passkey (WebAuthn) instead of a wallet key.
